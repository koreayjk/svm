How SVM works

Five promises that make going possible

$0room & board

Pay only for your flight

Housing, meals, ministry costs and local staff support are covered by the field through local giving. No organization fees.

1–12months

Stay as long as you want

Go for a month. Want to stay longer? Change your return flight. There is no extra cost for room and board.

3×a year

Go every break

Main sending seasons are December, January and May through August. Other times work too.

2nights a week

Trained, then sent

Sunday Mission Gathering plus Friday Training Night with DNA2 discipleship and PST evangelism.

3tracks

A lifelong path

Start short-term with Into the World. Go mid-term for one to two years. Some will give their lives to long-term missions and church planting.

For host fields

How hosting an SVM team works

For local churches and missionaries who want college students to serve with them. The field provides room and board through local giving. SVM sends trained, approved students.

What SVM sends

  • Trained students: weekly mission gathering and Friday training night
  • Language prepared: the core gospel message memorized in your language
  • Flexible stays: one month to a year, in your seasons

What the field provides

  • Housing and all meals
  • Ministry and activity costs
  • Local staff support and orientation

What you receive

  • Approved roster and arrival dates from SVM headquarters
  • Up to 5 English conversation classes a day, run by the team
  • Extension requests coordinated with you, airfare only for the student
  1. ConnectTalk with SVM about your ministry and needs
  2. Field pageWe set up your country page, photos and seasons
  3. Students applyThey train weekly and apply for your field
  4. Approved teamSVM approves and shares the roster and arrivals
  5. Serve togetherStudents arrive trained and ready to work with you
We protect your field. Students are told never to post local believers' faces, real names or exact church locations. Testimonies and photos are reviewed before they appear, and sensitive countries hide names and limit photos.

Want to host a team? Meet the SVM team at the Nov 20 launch, or ask any SVM leader to connect you with headquarters.
