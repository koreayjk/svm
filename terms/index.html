Simple rules for a safe community

By creating an account in the SVM app you agree to these terms and to our privacy notice.

Last updated October 2026

Who we are

The SVM app and this website are run by the Student Volunteer Movement in Bryan–College Station, Texas. The mobile apps are published by IM America Group Corp.

Your account

  • You must be at least 13 years old to use the app.
  • Use your real name and keep your password to yourself. You are responsible for what is posted from your account.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app: My → Delete account.

Community rules: zero tolerance

Chats, prayer requests, testimonies and team posts are shared with other members. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Do not post:

  • Harassment, bullying, threats or hate toward any person or group
  • Sexual, violent or otherwise inappropriate content
  • Spam, advertising or scams
  • Faces, real names or exact locations of local believers, or anything that could put people on the field at risk
  • Anything illegal or anyone else's private information

Reporting and blocking

Tap ⋯ next to any message or post to report it or block the person. Blocking hides their posts from you right away. SVM leaders review every report within 24 hours, remove content that breaks these rules and may suspend or remove the account that posted it.

What you post

You keep ownership of what you write and upload. You allow SVM to show it to other members in the app. Testimonies and support stories are reviewed by a leader before others can see them, and leaders may remove any content.

Applications and trips

  • Applying does not guarantee a place. SVM leaders and the host field decide on every team.
  • Every INTO THE WORLD participant must have personal travel medical insurance and upload the certificate at least 7 days before departure.
  • You are responsible for your passport, visa and flights, and for following the safety guidance of SVM and your host field.

The service

We work to keep the app available and accurate, but it is provided as is and may change. We may update these terms; if the changes are important, we will tell you in the app.

Questions

Talk to any SVM leader at the Sunday Mission Gathering, 2–4 PM, IM Friend Baptist Church, 408 E 24th St, Bryan, TX 77803.
