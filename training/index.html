Training

Trained for real,
then sent

Before anyone is sent, every student works through a clear path: four kinds of training, a final report, and one last step: preparing together with your field. You check off your progress in the SVM app, and your country leader confirms it. Both have to say yes before it counts.

1 · Language

Learn the core gospel message in the language of the nation you are going to, sentence by sentence.

2 · Explore · 7 lessons

Seven lessons from Via Nations on God’s Word, World and Work, studied with a few friends.

3 · Perspectives · 15 lessons

Fifteen lessons on the World Christian Movement through four lenses: biblical, historical, cultural and strategic.

4 · Perspectives sermons & notes

Listen to the 126-sermon Perspectives series and write your notes. A set number is required (30 for now).

5 · Final report

A final report on what you learned and how God is sending you. Your leader reads and confirms it.

6 · Prepared with your field

Before you go, we talk with the field to learn what is truly needed, then prepare the skills and resources to meet it.

1 · Language

Before students are sent, they learn the heart of the gospel in the language of the nation they are going to. It is a short, carefully chosen message, practiced one sentence at a time, so that on the field they can say it plainly and from memory.

In the app each sentence appears in the local script with a pronunciation guide and an English line. You choose the playback speed, loop it, and watch your mastery dots fill in as you practice. The app also counts how many days in a row you have practiced.

Four steps for every sentence

  1. Listen · Read along while it plays and let it loop.
  2. Shadow · Say it out loud with the voice.
  3. Hide · Cover the text and say it from memory.
  4. Self-test · Check yourself and pass each sentence.

2 · Explore · 7 lessons

Explore starts with questions many students quietly carry: what the Bible is really about, whether God is still at work in the world today, and whether their own life is meant to be part of something bigger. Over seven lessons it walks through God’s Word, God’s World and God’s Work.

Every lesson has the same five parts. Prep helps you look honestly at your own view. Read gives insight into what God is doing and how you can join him. Discuss is where the group studies the verses together. Live looks for new ways to act on what you believe. Pray lifts up peoples who have not yet heard of Jesus.

Along the way the guide introduces five “World Christian habits”:

  • Praying
  • Sending
  • Welcoming
  • Going
  • Mobilizing

  3. 3Praying to the Lord of the HarvestWhy intercession matters, and how your prayers join what God is already doing among the nations.p.27
  4. 4Sending Laborers into the Harvest FieldGoing and sending belong together. Learn why those who send, by praying, giving and caring, matter as much as those who go.p.39
  5. 5Welcoming the Nations Among UsMany people who have never heard of Jesus now live near us. Practical ways to befriend international neighbors and students.p.51

3 · Perspectives · 15 lessons

Perspectives on the World Christian Movement is a widely used course that walks through the whole story of God’s purpose for the nations. The study guide holds the fifteen lessons, and a companion reader gathers the readings that go with them.

Students move from why God sends people (the biblical lens), to how the church has spread and what is still left to do (historical), to how the gospel crosses cultures (cultural), to how new churches are started and multiplied (strategic).

4 · Perspectives sermons & notes

Our Sunday messages are preaching through the Perspectives book, 126 sermons in all. Students listen, and for each sermon they finish they write their own notes.

A set number of sermons must be finished before graduation, and the leader confirms each set of notes. In the app the sermons are numbered 1 to 126, and your progress shows at a glance: not yet, waiting for your leader, or confirmed.

How it works

  1. Listen to a sermon.
  2. Write your notes: what you heard, what stood out and how you will respond.
  3. Save them in the app. Each sermon has its own number, so you can pick up where you left off.
  4. Your leader reads the notes and confirms them. Editing later asks for a new confirmation.
  5. Repeat until you reach the required number (30 for now).

5 · Final report

After the sermons, each student writes a final report of at least a few paragraphs. It is a chance to put it all into words: what you learned from the lessons and sermons, how your view of the nations changed, and how God is sending you.

Your country leader reads it, confirms it and writes back with a comment. Once the language, Explore, Perspectives, sermons and report are all confirmed, you can request graduation, and the last step begins.

6 · Prepared with your field

Every field is different, so the last step of training depends on where you are going. Your country leader stays in touch with the church or missionary there to hear what they actually need: which skills would serve them, which resources would help, and what they would rather not have. Nothing is guessed from far away.

Then we prepare to meet that need together. You may practice a skill such as teaching English through conversation, gather what the field asked for, and train with your team and an experienced mentor. You go knowing what is expected, and the church on the field is ready to receive you.

How it works

  1. Your country leader talks with the church or missionary on the field.
  2. The field says what skills and resources would really help, and what would not.
  3. We prepare it together: the skill you will practice, the materials to bring, and your team and mentor.
  4. You train for that specific assignment before you leave.
  5. You go ready, and your leader keeps in touch with you and the field.

How it counts

  • You check each lesson, sermon and report in the app when you finish it
  • Your country leader looks it over and confirms it. Until then it shows as waiting
  • If you edit your notes or report afterwards, your leader confirms again
  • When everything is confirmed, you request graduation and a leader approves it

Joining part-way through?

You are welcome at any point. Join the group where it is now and make up the earlier lessons on your own time. Nothing is locked, and your leader can see where you are. We only ask that you do Lesson 1 of Explore first.

Lengths and requirements may change as we learn from the first groups.
