Privacy

Your information, kept small and kept safe

We collect only what we need to train and send students, and we never sell or share it for advertising.

Last updated October 2026

What we collect

  • Conference registration: your name, email, optional phone, and who you are (student, leader and so on).
  • SVM app account: your name and email, and if you add them, your school and phone.
  • Email updates: if you leave your email on the home page, we use it only to send SVM updates. Ask us any time and we will remove it.
  • What you do in the app: nations you sign, applications, attendance, training progress, messages, prayer requests, testimonies and the photos you upload.
  • Trip details: if you add them, your flights, who meets you and a local contact. Only you, your country leader and admins can see them.
  • Emergency help: when you press the emergency button, we alert your country leader and HQ with your note. Your location is included only if you tick “Include my location”, once, at that moment. We do not track your location.
  • Safety check-ins and notifications: when you are on a trip, the “I’m safe / I need help” check-ins you send, and, if you allow notifications, a device token so we can send them.

Why we use it

To send you conference updates, run the Sunday Mission Gathering, review applications, prepare teams and keep in touch before, during and after a trip. Nothing else.

Who can see it

  • SVM leaders see what they need to run the movement, such as applications and attendance.
  • Your host field receives only the approved team roster and arrival dates.
  • Other members see your first name and last initial on chats, prayers and name boards. On sensitive countries, only a count is shown.

Protecting people on the field

Please never post the faces, real names or exact locations of local believers. Leaders may remove content that could put anyone at risk.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase, a hosted database with access rules on every table. This site has no ads and no tracking cookies. YouTube videos load only after you press play.

Your choices

You can edit your profile in the app at any time, delete your own messages and posts, and block or report other members. To remove a conference registration, ask any SVM leader at the Sunday Mission Gathering and we will delete it.

Delete your account

In the SVM app, open My and tap Delete account at the bottom. Your profile, applications, insurance certificate, messages, prayer requests, testimonies, training progress and attendance are deleted right away and cannot be recovered. If you cannot sign in, ask any SVM leader and we will delete it for you.

Who runs SVM

The SVM app and this site are run by the Student Volunteer Movement in Bryan–College Station, Texas. The mobile apps are published by IM America Group Corp. Questions: talk to any SVM leader at the Sunday Mission Gathering, IM Friend Baptist Church, 408 E 24th St, Bryan, TX 77803.
